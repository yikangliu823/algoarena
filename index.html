Firebase setup (free, about 5 minutes, one time)
1. Go to console.firebase.google.com, sign in with your Google account, and click Add project (you can skip Analytics).
2. Left sidebar: Build > Realtime Database > Create Database > start in locked mode > choose us-central1 > Enable.
3. On the Realtime Database page, open the Rules tab, paste the rules below, and click Publish.
4. Gear icon (top left) > Project settings > Your apps > click the Web icon(</>) > Register app, then copy the firebaseConfig JSON.
5. Paste it below and click Save. This device will remember it.
